You're likely ready if you have stable income, a healthy credit score, savings for a down payment and closing costs, and a plan to stay in the area for several years. Our advisors can review your finances and help you get pre-approved so you know exactly what you can afford.

Spring and early summer usually bring the most buyers and the highest prices. That said, the best time also depends on your local market and personal situation, and our agents can advise you on the ideal timing for your area.

Your offer should be based on recent comparable sales, the home's condition, how long it has been listed, and current market competition. We prepare a detailed market analysis so you can make a strong, fair offer with confidence.

Closing costs are the fees paid to finalize a purchase, such as lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, and taxes. They typically range from 2% to 5% of the price. Buyers and sellers each pay certain costs, and some can be negotiated as part of the deal.
